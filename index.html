LinkedIn Positioning Review

Make the standard easier to understand.

Prepared for Alexander Coates. The strongest opportunity is to make The One Standard feel less like a broad leadership philosophy and more like the distinctive, practical development system that sits behind it.

The core shift From “helping people raise the standard” to clearly owning a practical 12-step system for leadership, culture and performance.
01 / WHAT I FOUND

The thinking is strong. The profile can make the product clearer.

The public brand already has a distinct idea, a named framework, a 12-step practice, individual and organisational applications, and a founder story grounded in real operational experience. The LinkedIn profile currently gives visitors more philosophy than product clarity.

01

Own the mechanism

“The One Standard” is the memorable asset. Bring the 12-step practice closer to the first line so the visitor understands what Alexander actually built.

02

Make the audience visible

The offer serves individuals, founders, managers, team leaders and organisations. The profile should make that breadth feel intentional rather than broad.

03

Use the lived proof

The frontline and team-leadership background is a powerful differentiator. It explains why the framework is practice-led rather than purely theoretical.

02 / CURRENT POSITIONING

What a new visitor sees today.

The profile establishes Alexander as the founder of The One Standard and explains the philosophy in depth. It is credible, but a visitor still has to connect the philosophy to the concrete product and use case.

Current headline

Founder & Creator, The One Standard Development | Helping people and organisations raise the standard they live, lead and perform by

What works
  • Founder ownership is clear.
  • The brand name is repeated.
  • The people and organisations audience is included.
  • The “standard” idea is consistent with the website.
What is still unclear
  • What exactly is The One Standard?
  • What does a buyer actually get?
  • Why should a leadership or people leader care?
  • What makes Alexander different from a general leadership coach or consultant?
03 / HEADLINE

Five stronger directions.

Each version makes a different tradeoff between product clarity, founder authority and organisational relevance.

OPTION 01

Founder, The One Standard | Helping people and organisations raise the standard they think, lead and perform by

Makes the offer immediately legible while keeping the core brand idea intact.

OPTION 02

Founder & Creator, The One Standard | A 12-step practice for stronger leadership, culture and performance

Turns the philosophy into a concrete product and outcome.

OPTION 03

Leadership & Culture Development | Founder of The One Standard | Build the standard. Change how teams perform.

Leans more toward organisational buyers and makes the business outcome clearer.

OPTION 04

Founder, The One Standard | Helping leaders build the standards that shape behaviour, culture and performance

Positions Alexander around the mechanism behind the problem, not generic leadership advice.

OPTION 05

The One Standard | Practical leadership and behavioural development built from real-world experience

A more understated, authority-led direction that foregrounds the grounded methodology.

04 / ABOUT

Keep the philosophy. Tighten the path to the offer.

The current About has a strong thesis and a credible personal story. The main improvement is structural: name the product earlier, define the format, clarify who it serves, then use Alexander's experience as proof of why the system exists.

Current About
Source profile
Success is rarely determined by talent alone. It is built, and sustained, by the standard we choose to live by. After more than two decades leading teams, developing people and studying many of the world's most respected thinkers, I've become convinced of one simple truth: Everything rises, or falls, to the standard we consistently uphold. I've seen capable people achieve extraordinary results once they became intentional about the standards they expected from themselves. I've also seen successful individuals and organisations lose momentum, not through a lack of ability, but through the gradual erosion of the standards that once made them exceptional. Those experiences led me to create The One Standard. Not simply as a leadership program, but as a practical philosophy supported by a structured framework that helps individuals and organisations intentionally raise the standard by which they think, lead, decide and perform. The framework combines lessons earned through my own experiences with timeless principles drawn from many of history's most respected minds in leadership, business, psychology, philosophy and human performance. My goal has been to bring these enduring ideas together into one practical system that creates lasting behavioural change through consistent practice. Because knowledge alone rarely changes lives. Practice does. The standards we practice become the standards we live by. Those standards shape our decisions, our behaviours and ultimately our results. I continue to work in frontline operational environments where leadership, accountability and performance are tested every day. My perspective isn't built solely from books or boardrooms. It's continually refined through real-world experience. I've also developed a physical movement practice founded on the same philosophy, that meaningful growth comes from small, intentional actions performed consistently over time. Here I share insights on leadership, culture, discipline, behavioural change and building environments where excellence becomes the expected standard. Whether you're committed to raising your own standard, leading exceptional people or creating a culture where excellence becomes the expectation, I'd welcome the opportunity to connect. "The standard you live by becomes the standard you inspire." Alexander Coates
New About
I believe sustainable performance is built on the standards people consistently uphold. I'm the founder of The One Standard, a practical 12-step development practice designed to help individuals and organisations raise the standard by which they think, lead, decide and perform. The idea came from more than 20 years of real-world experience across different industries, including frontline operational environments and leading teams under pressure. In large-scale agriculture, I was trusted to lead underperforming teams and turn them into stronger, more accountable teams through clear standards and consistent leadership. That experience shaped how I see development: Talent matters. Motivation matters. But the standards you repeatedly practise are what become your way of operating. The One Standard turns that idea into a structured practice. One short session each week for twelve weeks, carried into the work people are already doing. The goal is not another burst of motivation. It is lasting behavioural change through repetition. For individuals, it is a practical system for building greater discipline, clarity and personal capability. For organisations, it provides a scalable framework for developing people, strengthening culture and creating a shared standard across teams. I also continue to work in frontline operational environments, where leadership, accountability and performance are tested in real conditions. That keeps the work grounded in practice rather than theory. I write about leadership, culture, discipline, behavioural change and the standards that shape performance. If you are serious about raising the standard you live or lead by, connect with me here on LinkedIn.

DIRECTION 01

Feature the Complete Practice or a concise overview page. It gives a new profile visitor an immediate understanding of the product behind the philosophy.

DIRECTION 02

The Founder Story

Feature the About page or a short founder-led post explaining the frontline experiences that shaped the framework. This is the strongest credibility bridge between Alexander and the methodology.

DIRECTION 03

Feature the free self-assessment, practice overview, or app preview. The goal is to let visitors experience the method instead of only reading about it.
